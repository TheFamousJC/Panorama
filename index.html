PANORAMA GUIDE & FAQ (PLAIN ENGLISH)
What is Panorama in simple words?
Panorama is a private daily scratchpad with a personal analyst built into it. Dump raw thoughts during the week, and hit Connect The Dots on Friday to see patterns, connect ideas, and get clear action steps.
Where are my thoughts stored?
Notes are saved in this browser's localStorage. Gemini review sends notes, North Star, and recent snag history to Google. Sharing with JC's Dot only copies your chosen context; you paste it yourself. Fonts load from Google, and browser dictation may use an online speech service. The PIN is a screen lock, not encryption.
What if Google AI shows a "High Demand" error?
Gemini review stops after 7 seconds. You can try again or use Share with JC's Dot to copy selected context to your assistant without a Gemini key.
