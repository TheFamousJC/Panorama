          Frequently asked questions

          What does Panorama do?

          Panorama is a place to collect ideas, worries, and things to think through. When you want a fresh perspective, take selected thoughts to an AI assistant and bring back a review of patterns and possible next steps. You choose what to act on.

          What is My Dot? Do I need one?

          My Dot means your personal AI assistant in ChatGPT. Availability may vary; not everyone has a Dot. Panorama does not create or connect one for you. You can also use the prepared message in a regular ChatGPT conversation or another assistant that can follow its reply format. No account, setup, or API key is needed to save thoughts here.

          How do I share thoughts and bring back a review?
          1. Choose Share with My Dot, select thoughts, and decide whether to include your priority or recent review themes.
          2. Copy the prepared message. Open ChatGPT, sign in if prompted, and paste it into your Dot conversation or another assistant conversation.
          3. Copy the complete structured reply back to Panorama. Choose Preview review, check it, then Save review.

          Nothing is sent automatically. Panorama cannot see your login, read your chats, or retrieve the reply for you.

          Why won't Panorama read my assistant's reply?

          The copied message asks for a structured reply called JSON. Copy the entire reply, including any code block. If the assistant adds a chatty explanation instead, ask it to return only the JSON requested in the original message. Previewing never changes your saved review.

          What is Review with Google AI?

          It is a separate optional route under Settings > Other review options. It needs a Google API key and available quota; provider charges may apply. Running it sends all current thoughts, your priority, and recent review context to Google. It does not contact My Dot. Optional drafting sends the selected suggestion to Google.

          Where are my thoughts saved? Do they sync?

          Thoughts, reviews, and History are saved in this browser for this site. They do not automatically sync to another device, browser, profile, or site address. Clearing browser data can remove them; private browsing may discard them when you close it. Download a backup to keep a separate copy.

          How do backup and restore work?

          In Settings > Backup & restore, choose Download backup. Keep the file somewhere private. It includes thoughts, reviews, History, and your priority, but not your API key or PIN. To move data, open Panorama in the other browser and restore that file. Restore replaces the information included in the backup; download your current backup first if you want to keep it.

          What should I know about privacy?

          Share only what you are comfortable giving your assistant. Copied thoughts enter your clipboard; after you paste or run a Google AI review, the provider's privacy settings apply. Voice input may use an online speech service. Saved browser data is not encrypted by Panorama. The optional PIN hides the screen; it does not protect files from someone with access to your browser profile.

          What happens to previous reviews and archived thoughts?

          Saving a different review keeps the previous one in History. Archive these thoughts keeps the current thoughts and review in History, then clears your current list. Your priority stays. Deleting thoughts is a separate action; nothing is scheduled or sent on your behalf.
