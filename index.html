          Your data is saved in this browser, not synced between devices. Sharing with My Dot is manual: you choose what to copy and paste. Your assistant's privacy settings apply. Voice input may use an online speech service.

          The optional PIN hides your desk; it does not encrypt your data or protect it from someone with access to this browser profile.
