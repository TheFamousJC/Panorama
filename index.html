PANORAMA GUIDE & FAQ (PLAIN ENGLISH)
What is Panorama in simple words?
Panorama is a private, zero-friction daily scratchpad with a personal analyst built into it. During the week, you dump raw, messy thoughts—bugs, sudden travel plans, work friction, or random ideas. Whenever you're ready (usually Friday), the AI reads everything at once to show you recurring patterns, connect related ideas, and suggest simple next steps.
Where are my thoughts stored? Is this private?
100% private and local. Everything is saved directly in your browser's private computer memory (LocalStorage). Your thoughts never go to any remote server or cloud database unless you run an AI review using your own personal Google AI Studio key.
Do I have to organize or tag my notes manually?
No! You can simply type a thought and hit Enter (or press the microphone button to dictate). While you can click a quick tag like "Annoyance" or "Work", the AI understands the meaning of your notes regardless of tags.
What happens when I click "Finish Week & Start Fresh"?
It snapshots your current week and saves it safely into your "Past Weeks" archive. To make sure you don't drop important balls, any open action steps or unanswered questions are automatically carried forward into Monday's scratchpad.
How does the "PIN Security" feature work?
Click "PIN Security" in the top bar to set a 4 to 8 digit number code. If enabled, whenever you open or refresh Panorama, the page blurs and blocks access until the PIN is entered.
Do I need to pay for Google Gemini?
No. Google AI Studio provides a free Gemini API key for personal prototyping. Simply click "Get Free Key ↗" in the top bar, copy your key, and paste it once into the top bar. Panorama runs on the high-speed Flash tier which is fast and completely free under normal personal limits.
Can I use this without any API key at all?
Yes! Click "Use in Claude / ChatGPT (Copy Prompt)". It compiles all your active thoughts into a formatted request that you can paste directly into Claude, ChatGPT, or any other assistant for free. Then paste the reply back in to display it visually.
